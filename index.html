About AQUANOVA

AQUANOVA is a smart and sustainable aquaponics system that combines IoT monitoring, automation and digital twin technology to improve water management, fish health and plant growth.

Sensors continuously monitor important water and environmental parameters.

Intelligent control can help manage pumps and system conditions automatically.
